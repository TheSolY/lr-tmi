Low Resource Reconstruction Attacks Through Benign Prompts

Sol Yarkoni, Roi Livni
School of Electrical & Computer Engineering, Tel Aviv University

This project investigates the risks of unintended data memorization in generative models. Our attack successfully reconstructs training-set images—including real identifiable individuals—using benign prompts alone.
